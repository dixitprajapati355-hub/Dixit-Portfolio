05 / About

A designer
who looks
closely.

Dixit is a communication designer working across branding, strategy, positioning and visual identity.

I am interested in the point where an idea becomes visible — where research becomes a decision, a decision becomes a system, and a system becomes something people can feel.

Outside client work, I keep making: calligraphy, photography, pottery, drawing and visual experiments. Not as a list of skills, but as ways of staying curious.
